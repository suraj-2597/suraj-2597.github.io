Projects

fraud-signalpython · sklearn

Identity fraud detection on credit applications. The interesting part is the feature engineering: fraud signal lives in cross-field relationships (email age vs. stated age, thin file + VOIP phone), not in any single column.

Evaluated at review-capacity operating points instead of accuracy. The per-segment slice showed the model catches 93% of synthetic identities but only 43% of first-party fraud at a 5% review rate — which told me that segment needs different data, not a better classifier.

neural-query-enginepython · sqlite

Document search pipeline: sentence-aware chunking with overlap, TF-IDF vectors, cosine retrieval with metadata filtering. Built as the retrieval layer for a RAG setup; kept separate because retrieval is where the quality problems actually were.

Queries answer in 2–5ms on a small corpus. Two small ranking changes (sublinear TF, a first-chunk position bonus) measurably beat everything else I tried.
